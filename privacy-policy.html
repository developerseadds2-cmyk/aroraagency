Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Arora Agency, Ludhiana

1. Introduction

Welcome to Arora Agency ("we", "our", or "us"). We are an authorized distribution firm based in Ludhiana, Punjab, operating in the supply of home appliances, pressure cookers, crockery, glassware, cutlery, and domestic lifestyle products. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any information you share with us.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us, visit our website, or send inquiries regarding product availability, dealerships, or bulk orders, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Your full name, mobile number, email address, and business name.
  • Inquiry Details: Product categories of interest (e.g., Havells, Prabha Steel, Roxx Crockery, Pigeon, Yera Glass) and any customized messages or requirements.
  • Technical Data: Basic browser parameters, IP address, and interaction data collected solely for website performance and security purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly for legitimate commercial and customer service purposes:

  • To respond to dealership and retail product inquiries.
  • To provide updated product catalogs, rate sheets, and quotation estimates.
  • To deliver customer support and warranty assistance related to our distributed brands.
  • To maintain security and prevent unauthorized access or abuse of our communication channels.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or contact details with third-party advertisers or unauthorized external entities. Information is only shared under the following conditions:

  • With logistics partners or transport agencies for dispatching product shipments.
  • With official brand manufacturers (such as Havells, Prabha Steel, etc.) when required for warranty validation or manufacturer service calls.
  • When mandated by applicable Indian laws, judicial orders, or legal proceedings.

5. Data Security

We implement standard organizational and digital measures to safeguard your inquiry data from loss, unauthorized disclosure, or misuse. Our website employs secure protocols to transmit your inquiries safely.

6. Your Rights

You may request correction, updates, or deletion of your contact records from our inquiry database at any time by contacting our Ludhiana office directly via phone or email.
